**The idea.** Four Majorana zero modes on two gate-defined InAs–superconductor wires (`majorana`) hold one qubit at fixed total fermion parity (`enc_parity`, the tetron): Z and X are parities of different mode pairs. Gates would be parity-measurement sequences read by rf quantum capacitance (`g_mbq`, `ro_qcap`). The claim is a topological gap: with the modes far apart, their splitting falls exponentially with wire length and only an arriving quasiparticle flips a parity. Table 8.3's bet, protection in hardware instead of a code, is an existence claim, at the price of Clifford-only gates and no fallback.

**What it needs.** One chain, no alternates: the gate *requires* the wires and the readout, every gate being a measurement; the encoding two wires per tetron; the readout the island's quantum capacitance; the wires an InAs–Pb stack from III-V MBE (`fab_mbe`), whose disorder can mimic Majorana signatures. No *replaces* or *conflicts* edge: with no rival at any layer, one failure strands the path. Code, decoder and interconnect are empty (§7.7); a Floquet or measurement-based code awaits a demonstrated qubit. The gate is an empty slot: specified since 2008, never operated.

**Where it stands.** One machine, no device: Majorana 1 (and Majorana 2) (Microsoft), contested, 5/30 in §2.7 [D]. Measured: single-wire parity readout at 1% assignment error, not by itself proof of Majorana modes, the editors noted [D][T1][T2]; tetron Z- and X-loop parity lifetimes of 12.4 ms and 14.5 µs, the X–Z succession test deferred [P][T3]; a ~20 s InAs–Pb parity switching time in one wire of one tetron, called a qubit lifetime by Microsoft's blog [P][T4][C][T5]. No braiding, X measurement on Majorana 2, or two-qubit operation. A Nature Matters Arising disputes the topological reading, answered without concession [D][T8]; QuTech's independent Kitaev-chain parity qubit has limited protection [P][T7].

**Exceptions and borrowings.** None off-slot: the machine fills all seven stations and fits no other path — weak evidence: the path was drawn around it; one machine cannot test its width. The code gap (`∅G-pathcode`) is telling: Microsoft's own geometric codes need all-to-all connectivity, which its neutral-atom product offers (§3.3) and the intended tetron lattice does not. Of its own stations only MBE travels: Belenos and Lucy (Quandela), on the fusion path, grow quantum-dot photon sources with it.

**The next test.** Per §5.2, an X-parity lifetime near the Z lifetime, or any two-qubit entanglement, confirms; neither by end-2027 demotes. Since 22 September 2026 DARPA's evaluators have on-site access to a Majorana 2 system in Microsoft's Maryland research centre — access, not a result [C][T10]. Expectation: by March 2028 an X/Z lifetime ratio below 10² (now ~10³ [T3]) or a joint parity measurement across two tetrons, confirmed by DARPA; without either, the path is a materials result, not an architecture.
