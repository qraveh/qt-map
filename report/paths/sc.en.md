**The idea.** A transmon (`transmon`) is a capacitively shunted Josephson junction whose two lowest levels serve bare as the qubit (`enc_bare`). The architecture tiles transmons in a lithographic lattice (`cx_nn`), switches neighbour exchange with flux-tuned couplers (`g_tc`), drives them from room-temperature racks (`ct_rt`), reads them dispersively (`ro_disp`) and corrects with the rotated surface code, whose weight-4 checks are the lattice's own neighbourhoods. A circuit atom is drawn, not found: gates take tens of nanoseconds [G] and the QEC cycle 1.1 µs [D][S2], the map's fastest clock (§7.4). The price is coherence (T₁ 68 µs on Willow [D][S1]), identical qubits and any coupling beyond the neighbour. The bet: speed and wafer fabrication outrun the error budget; links between chips come later.

**What it needs.** Everything rests on `fab_sc`: the transmon *requires* junction lithography, so its frequency spread is fixed at fabrication; the trimming that repairs it has no station yet. The surface code *requires* only neighbour checks. Bivariate-bicycle codes keep 12 logical qubits in 288 physical where the surface code needs nearly 3,000, but need a degree-6 graph [S][S42]: `code_qldpc` *requires* long-range couplers (`cx_lr`) and *conflicts* with `cx_nn`, open since 2024-03. Readout and reset are two-thirds of the 651 ns derived round [S] (§7.4). Four control stations *replace* one another; SFQ (`ct_sfq`) *conflicts* with the transmon, its switching photons breaking Cooper pairs: 0.96(2)% of a 1.2(1)% error per Clifford in 2023 [D][S43]. Encoding has one option; the microwave–optical transducer slot is empty.

**Where it stands.** 75 of the register's 160 machines and 54 devices (Table 8.3): 35 deployed, 14 demonstrated, 6 retired, 17 announced or planned, 3 other; IBM supplies 19 rows, China 16. The largest gate-capable device remains IBM Quantum Osprey, 433 qubits (2022); Condor's 1,121 never reached users. The best 2Q error, 1.0×10⁻³ on Origin Wukong-180 (4th gen), is a company figure [C] (Table 8.3); Willow's device mean is 99.88% [D][S1]. Willow ran d=7 at Λ = 2.14 [D][S2], then 7.72×10⁻⁴ per cycle (Jul 2026) [D][S11]; Zuchongzhi 3.2 d=7 at Λ = 1.40 [D][S12]. Code, decoder and interconnect slots are empty on 59, 67 and 57 machines; 12 rows publish no gate mechanism; 311 of 812 evidence cells are documented.

**Exceptions and borrowings.** No machine falls off the slots or fits another architecture better; alternates carry the direction. Wiring leaves the chip edge (`ct_vio`) on RIKEN 64, RIKEN–Fujitsu 256, OQC Toshiko and QuantWare VIO-40K, racks still warm; control goes cold only in plans or at five qubits: SFQ on SEEQC's system (single-qubit gates only) and QuamCore (design only), cryo-CMOS as alternate on Heron r2, Starling and Blue Jay. Sycamore and Zuchongzhi stack dies (`ic_multidie`); modules (`ic_mcm`) ship only on Rigetti Cepheus, at 99.6%→99.1% [C][S6]. IBM's utility line sits in mitigation (`code_mitig`: Eagle, Heron r3, Nighthawk r2) and detection (`code_detect`: Falcon, Heron r2/r3, Origin Wukong 3rd gen); qLDPC runs only on Kunlun. Nominal members: Osprey, Eagle, Condor, Falcon and LBNL AQT use cross-resonance (`g_cr`), not tunable couplers; targets (Amaravati, CSCS, KRISS 50); plans (Kookaburra to Blue Jay, Fujitsu 10,000, Google fluxonium); component testbeds (Toshiba coupler, Loon); undisclosed stacks (Tianyan-504, RIKEN–Fujitsu 256, Toshiko). Gap cells show the architecture drawn too narrowly: a central-resonator bus (IQM Star 24, VLQ, DRDO–TIFR), leakage-removal units (Willow, Zuchongzhi 3.2), post-fabrication frequency trimming (Willow, Cepheus, Academia Sinica). Dual-rail borrows `transmon`, `ct_vio` and `ic_mcm`; cat `ic_multidie`; spins `ct_cryocmos`.

**The next test.** Expectations to 31 Mar 2028 (§5.2). A bivariate-bicycle memory on c-couplers, Kookaburra or a Kunlun successor, reports a logical error per cycle below its physical idle error; Kunlun's 8.91% [P][S32] is the baseline. If Kookaburra is undelivered again by end-2027, the qLDPC branch is demoted and the architecture stays a surface-code machine at >10³ physical qubits per logical at 10⁻⁶ [S] (§2.1). And a gate-capable device above 433 qubits with published two-qubit errors, plus SFQ or cryo-CMOS control of ≥50 qubits; if neither appears and readout stays near 10⁻², scale by fabrication is demoted to scale by modules.
