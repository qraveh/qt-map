**The idea.** Single photons in path or time-bin encoding are never joined by a gate: Linear-optical fusion (`g_fusion`) measures two photons from small pre-made resource states in the Bell basis, destroying both, and Fusion-based fault tolerance reads the logical state from the pattern of outcomes. The fact exploited: a photon does not decohere, loss is its only error, and a failed fusion announces itself: an erasure at a known place. The price is determinism. The bet: a foundry can build fault tolerance if each photon loses under 2.7% end to end, the threshold with 6-ring resource states [S][P10].

**What it needs.** Fusion *requires* number-resolving detection to herald and switching with feed-forward to route the next photon within its flight time; the switches *require* the Photonic IC foundry, where each costs 0.1–0.2 dB of a ~0.5 dB per-photon budget [D][P1]. The code *requires* the Multi-photon resource-state factory (`src_resource`), an empty slot: fused states reach 8 photons at under 1 Hz against 24–168 photons at MHz [D] (§7.7). A 2026 re-analysis adds a logical-error floor set by fusion failure alone [S][P23]. Seven layers hold one station each, and nothing inside the architecture *replaces* or *conflicts*; fusion itself forecloses the continuous-variable branch, *replacing* Gaussian gates as counting *replaces* homodyne detection.

**Where it stands.** Five machines, three devices (Table 8.3), no logical qubit. Omega chipset (PsiQuantum, February 2025), all ten stations in slot, is a component set and is counted as such, not as a device: fusion Bell fidelity 99.22%, chip-to-chip Bell 99.72% over 42 m [D][P1]; its 7.8×10⁻³ is that single fusion, and its code and decoder cells are flagged no-QEC-cycle. Belenos and Lucy (Quandela, 12 qubits each; Lucy at CEA, 2025 [P7]) are the largest gate-capable devices; their 6.2×10⁻² is Ascella's 2023 figure [D][P24], and Lucy has 0 of 14 cells documented. The ETRI 8-photon silicon-photonic qubit chip publishes no error rate; Carina (QuiX Quantum, 8 qubits) awaits commissioning at DLR [P8].

**Exceptions and borrowings.** No cell falls off the architecture, yet it is drawn wider than its bet. Belenos, Lucy and the ETRI chip occupy the fusion station with post-selected or probabilistic gates in a reconfigurable mesh, circuit-model linear optics that the register's own cells call "not FBQC fusion"; only Omega and Carina fuse. Quandela's fault-tolerance design is a hybrid of quantum emitters and linear-optical gates [P25]. Both Quandela machines carry an Atlas gap, `∅G-twofab`: a III-V quantum-dot source and a SiN/silica mesh, two fabrication stations where the map allows one (layer 10 too narrow). Omega and Belenos list the resource-state factory, an empty slot, as an alternate station; Belenos's own cell calls the route undemonstrated (mislabelled). The other four have no code, decoder or interconnect: early stage. On other architectures, Aurora, Jiuzhang 4.0 and Borealis use its detector station.

**The next test.** By March 2028 (§5.2), a chain of three or more fusions, or a resource state beyond Bell pairs fused into a network, published with an end-to-end success rate, would show that the architecture's real clock, resource-state supply, exists. Demote if only single-fusion figures remain, fused states stay at eight photons, PsiQuantum's Stage C validation [P12] comes back negative, or its 2027 target moves.
