**The idea.** Single photons in path or time-bin encoding are never joined by a gate: Linear-optical fusion (`g_fusion`) measures two photons from small pre-made resource states in the Bell basis, destroying both, and Fusion-based fault tolerance reads the logical state from the pattern of outcomes. The fact exploited: a photon does not decohere, loss is its dominant error, and a failed fusion announces itself: it erases one of its two measured outcomes, at a known place. The price is determinism. The bet: a foundry can build fault tolerance if each photon loses under 2.7% end to end, the threshold with 6-ring resource states [S][P10].

**What it needs.** Fusion *requires* number-resolving detection to herald and switching with feed-forward to route the next photon within its flight time; the switches *require* the Photonic IC foundry, where each costs 0.1–0.2 dB of a ~0.5 dB per-photon budget [D][P1]. The code *requires* the Multi-photon resource-state generator (`src_resource`), an empty slot: fused states reach 8 photons at under 1 Hz against 24–168 photons at MHz [D] (§7.6). A 2026 re-analysis adds a logical-error floor set by fusion failure alone [S][P23]. Seven layers hold one technology each, and nothing inside the architecture *replaces* or *conflicts*; fusion itself forecloses the continuous-variable branch, *replacing* Gaussian gates as counting *replaces* homodyne detection.

Omega chipset (PsiQuantum, February 2025), all ten technologies in slot, is a component set and is counted as such, not as a device: fusion Bell fidelity 99.22%, chip-to-chip Bell 99.72% over 42 m [D][P1]; its 7.8×10⁻³ is that single fusion, and its code and decoder cells are flagged no-QEC-cycle. Belenos and Lucy (Quandela, 12 qubits each; Lucy at CEA, 2025 [P7]) are the largest gate-capable devices; their 6.2×10⁻² is Ascella's 2023 figure [D][P24], and Lucy has {{N_M_QUANDELA_LUCY_CELLS_OK}} of {{N_M_QUANDELA_LUCY_CELLS}} cells documented. The ETRI 8-photon silicon-photonic qubit chip publishes no error rate; Carina (QuiX Quantum, 8 qubits) awaits commissioning at DLR [P8]; Quandela's Canopus (24 qubits) is announced for its cloud in 2026 [C][P41].

**Exceptions and borrowings.** No cell falls off the architecture, yet it is drawn wider than its bet. Belenos, Lucy and the ETRI chip occupy the fusion technology with post-selected or probabilistic gates in a reconfigurable mesh, circuit-model linear optics that the register's own cells call "not FBQC fusion"; only Omega and Carina fuse. Quandela's fault-tolerance design is a hybrid of quantum emitters and linear-optical gates [P25]. Both Quandela machines carry an Atlas gap, `∅G-twofab`: a III-V quantum-dot source and a SiN/silica mesh, two fabrication technologies where the map allows one (layer 10 too narrow). Omega and Belenos list the resource-state generator, an empty slot, as an alternate technology; Belenos's own cell calls the route undemonstrated (mislabelled). The other four have no code, decoder or interconnect: early stage. On other architectures, Aurora, Jiuzhang 4.0 and Borealis use its detector technology.

**The next test.** By March 2028 (§5.2), a chain of three or more fusions, or a resource state beyond Bell pairs fused into a network, published with an end-to-end success rate, would show that the architecture's real clock, resource-state supply, exists. Demoted in October 2026: PsiQuantum's 2027 target moved — its June 2026 release dates only the cryoplant (second half of 2027) and names no operating date. Promoted back by a dated first system or a fusion-based logical qubit; demoted further if only single-fusion figures remain, fused states stay at eight photons, or PsiQuantum's Stage C validation [P12] comes back negative.

**What the builders say.** PsiQuantum's co-founder, Jeremy O'Brien, argues that a useful quantum computer must fully leverage the semiconductor industry [C][P30]. In 2024 PsiQuantum planned its Brisbane site operational by the end of 2027, its first system near a million physical qubits [R][P31]; in June 2026, breaking ground at a new site in Moreton Bay, it set cryoplant delivery for the second half of 2027, with commissioning and chip cabinets to follow, naming no operating date [R][P32]. Quandela plans 100+ logical qubits in 2030–31 [R][P33] and says hundreds of logical qubits will draw under 500 kW [C][P33]. QuiX promised a universal machine in 2026 and error correction in 2027 [R][P34]; Carina's core hardware, designed for a universal gate set, reached DLR in July 2026 [C][P35].
