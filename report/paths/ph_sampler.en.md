**The idea.** Squeezed light or single photons enter a Programmable linear-optical interferometer (`g_lointer`) and are counted at its outputs; the output probabilities are hafnians of matrices built from the network (permanents for single photons), #P-hard to compute [G][P28]. No entangling gate, no feed-forward, no qubit: the only claim is that no classical computer reproduces the samples (goal G1). Where the fusion architecture builds qubits from small resource states and the continuous-variable architecture encodes them in GKP grids, this architecture skips the qubit. The bet: at high photon number the statistics outrun classical samplers before loss hands the output back to them.

**What it needs.** The interferometer *requires* light in the mesh and room-temperature electro-optic phase shifters; Bulk-optics and fibre assembly (`fab_bulk`) *requires* fibre-coupled modulators and delay loops. The carriers *replace* each other, as do photon counting and homodyne detection, and bulk assembly and the photonic IC foundry. Everything else is transmission: noisy Gaussian boson sampling becomes efficiently simulable once squeezing, transmission and detector efficiency fall below a bound [S][P29]. Code, decoder and interconnect are ∅ by design (§7.3): a sample has no logical state to protect, so loss is modelled rather than corrected, and classical challengers exploit it [P6].

**Where it stands.** Three machines, three devices, none gate-capable (Table 8.3). Jiuzhang 4.0 (USTC, May 2026): 1,024 squeezed states in 8,176 modes, up to 3,050 detected photons, 51% system efficiency, one sample per 25.6 µs [D][P6]. Borealis (Xanadu, 2022): 216 squeezed modes through three fibre loops, TES number-resolving detectors, up to 219 photons, one sample per 36 µs [D][P27]; the register flags its claim eroded. PT-2 (ORCA, deployed 2024–26) samples photon-subtracted squeezed states in a two-loop time-bin interferometer with a number-resolving detector [P][P26]; ORCA publishes no mode count, clock or loss. For the register the ∅ layers mean three things: no sampler enters the gate, code or decoder counts; their nine none cells in layers 7–9 are the architecture, not a delay, and inflate Table 8.2's maturity profile; three of photonics' eight devices (Table 8.1) are samplers, so the family's device count overstates its processors.

**Exceptions and borrowings.** Jiuzhang 4.0 and Borealis hold CV Gaussian gates, the continuous-variable architecture's technology, as an off-slot layer-3 alternate: a Gaussian interferometer without homodyne feed-forward. That is lineage, not mislabelling; Borealis's successor is Aurora. Both leave layer 2 none. The encoding technology, Time-bin / path photonic encoding, rests on PT-2 alone and fits badly: PT-2 fills every time bin, nominally one photon each [P][P26], so its bins are modes, not the two-bin qubit the technology defines; and the technology *requires* a single-photon carrier, while PT-2's is squeezed light. No machine uses the single-photon or homodyne alternates either: in layers 1, 2 and 6 the architecture is drawn wider than its register.

**The next test.** By 31 December 2027: no published classical sampler matches Jiuzhang 4.0 on its own benchmarks, and ORCA publishes PT-3's modes, loops and loss with a classical comparison. Demote if a classical algorithm matches Jiuzhang 4.0, as one matched each earlier advantage claim, or if PT-3, due in 2026 and undelivered (§2.5), ships without those figures.

**What the builders say.** USTC presents Jiuzhang 4.0 as more than a sampler: Gaussian boson sampling can also generate bosonic error-correcting codes [D][P6], and control of thousands of photons, it says, reaches far beyond classical tractability and opens a path to fault-tolerant photonic hardware [D][P6]. ORCA's case is time multiplexing: fibre delays let each photon source be reused many times [C][P38]. In May 2025 its chief executive expected quantum advantage within 24 months, from PT-3, then due in early 2026 [R][P39]; in June 2026 ORCA installed a PT-2 at a Japanese enterprise and scheduled its upgrade to PT-3 for later in the year [R][P40].
