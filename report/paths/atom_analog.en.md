**The idea.** Keep the alkali tweezer array with its lasers, transport and imaging; drop the gate: global Rydberg lasers drive the whole register under one Ising Hamiltonian, Analog Rydberg Hamiltonian evolution (`g_rydanalog`) on the Ground–Rydberg analog qubit (`enc_gr`). Waveforms Ω(t), Δ(t) and atom geometry are the program, a bit-string snapshot the output. The bet: for many-body physics (G1), native Hamiltonian evolution reaches hundreds of atoms now. It gives up memory, gates and any route to fault tolerance.

**What it needs.** The evolution *requires* the ground–Rydberg basis, a Rydberg level in the carrier and global Rydberg lasers; imaging, an imaging transition. Code, decoder and interconnect are empty by design (§7.7): here analog simulation stops being a quantum computer in the register's sense. The encoding is a basis, not a memory: |r⟩ cannot be shelved; Aquila's T2* is 5.8 µs, programs stop at 4 µs [C][N40]; errors integrate coherently with no instant for a syndrome, so no code attaches and no decoder has input. Without an entangling gate they are devices, not processors (Table 8.3 prints no gate-capable size, no two-qubit error). The one *replaces* edge swaps the species.

**Where it stands.** Three machines, three devices, none gate-capable. Aquila (QuEra, 256 Rb-87 atoms, on Amazon Braket since November 2022) [C][N40] carries the physics: (2+1)D string breaking in a gauge theory, no advantage claim [D][A6]. Fresnel / Fresnel 2 (Pasqal, deployed 2023–2024) publishes no error rates. QUIONE (ICFO, April 2024) images an ⁸⁴Sr quantum gas site by site in an 813.4 nm lattice, fidelity above 94% [D][N41]. §7.4's T₂ for the path is the ground–Rydberg qubit's own 11.4 µs (Aquila, spin echo) [C][N40] since 26 Sep 2026, no longer the carrier's 12.6-s caesium hyperfine record [N6], which a qubit with a Rydberg level cannot use. 25 of 33 cells documented. No machine postdates 2024; the vendors' later ones (Gemini-class, Libra, Orion, Vela) are gate-model.

**Exceptions and borrowings.** The dual-mode Pasqal machines mirror each other: Fresnel / Fresnel 2 runs analog as primary, its digital mode (Hyperfine / clock-state qubit, Rydberg-blockade CZ) and PIC-generated tweezers as alternates; Orion Alpha / Beta ("Ruby") / Gamma, on the alkali path, swaps the modes; all else matches. Each fits its own path seven stations to five: the boundary with atom_rb is a mode switch in layers 2–3 of one hardware (hybrid). The gate separates simulator from processor; the line fault tolerance needs, a code, neither has crossed. QUIONE lacks encoding, gate and Rydberg state: a Bose–Hubbard gas in a lattice (`∅G-lattice`, shared with MAQCS) under a carrier named for erasure-native tweezer qubits, here by elimination (four stations in slot): the path is too narrow for lattice quantum-gas simulators. Caltech's erasure-excising Sr Rydberg simulator [N9], which the new alkaline-earth edges describe, is not in the register.

**The next test.** Two expectations; §5.2 has no analog row. Confirmation: by 31 December 2027 a commercial analog machine publishes a many-body fidelity at 60 or more atoms or a T2* above 20 µs, or an analog result survives a year of classical challenge (§2.8). Demotion: if Vela and QuEra's next systems ship without analog specifications and no analog-first machine is announced by mid-2027, the path is a mode, analog alternates of layers 2–3 on atom_rb, not an architecture.
